How it works

Three steps, one conversation.

02

Where we go

Three coasts,
one of them home.

Portugal

Home coast · Best autumn to spring

Consistent Atlantic swell, more coastline than you could surf in a season, and a beach for every level within a short drive.

Brazil

Where he's from · Best months to confirm

Warm water, no wetsuit, long empty beach breaks — the easiest place to spend a week getting better.

Spain

A short hop north · Best autumn

The answer when the swell is wrong in Portugal, and good enough on its own to be the reason for the trip.

What's handled

Everything
except the flights.

  • The coast and the week, chosen to fit your dates and level
  • Somewhere good to stay, booked for you
  • Airport transfers, and a lift to the water every day
  • Boards and wetsuits
  • Coaching in the water, every day
  • Video review, if you want it
