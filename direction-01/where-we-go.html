Where we go

Three coasts
we actually know.

We could list twenty countries. We list three.

These are the ones Jobson has surfed enough to plan properly — where he knows which beach works on which wind, and who to call when a plan needs changing on the morning.

If you have somewhere else in mind, ask anyway. If we don't know it well enough to do it well, we'll say so.

Home coast · Best months to confirm

Portugal

Jobson has taught here for years, which means the plan changes with the forecast instead of the brochure. Consistent Atlantic swell, a coastline long enough that there is almost always somewhere working, and beaches that suit a first week as easily as a hard one.
