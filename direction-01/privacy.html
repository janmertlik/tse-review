Privacy

What we do with your details.

Draft — not legal advice This is a plain-English starting point covering what the site actually does. Before launch it needs a real legal entity name, a contact address and a review by someone qualified — the audience is European, so GDPR applies.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, your email, and whatever you tell us about the trip you have in mind. If you join the group-trip list, just your email.

Why we have it

To answer you. If you asked about a trip, we use your details to reply, to plan it and to talk to you about it. If you joined the list, we use your email once, to tell you when the first dates are set.

What we don't do

We don't sell your details, share them with anyone who isn't arranging your trip, or add you to a marketing list you didn't ask for. There is no advertising tracking on this site.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for a period to be confirmed so we can pick up a conversation where it left off. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — any time, by email. We'll do it within a month.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If that changes, this page changes first and you'll be asked before anything non-essential is set.

Who to contact

Legal entity name, address and contact email to be confirmed.
